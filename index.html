A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Not sure where to start? Here are some things you might be wondering.

Ask us a question ↗
Do I need an appointment before visiting? +

Booking ahead is recommended to confirm your preferred time and treatment availability. You can send an enquiry using the form below.

I'm nervous about seeing a dentist. Is that okay? +

Of course. Let the team know what worries you so they can discuss your concerns and help make your appointment comfortable.

How much will my treatment cost? +

Indicative prices are shown in the treatment catalogue. The clinic will advise the final cost after assessing your needs.

Can I ask about braces or dental implants? +

Yes. You can enquire about braces or implants through WhatsApp. The clinic can explain consultation requirements and treatment suitability.

Is a booking confirmed after sending the form? +

No. The form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Your booking is only confirmed when the clinic replies and agrees on a date and time.
